## Approval and date disposition

The accepted target and missed 2026-10-08 date remain historical facts; replacement acceptance and integration date remain unset. The November 13 and December 14 proposals are superseded as active milestones. Date acceptance requires the complete achievable target/command and owner commitments; it cannot be inferred from approving a guarantee or allocating money.

The existing OpenBao service is only the first candidate to assess after compatible scope approval; an ordinary key service in the existing cloud is the second conditional assessment path if safe OpenBao operation is impractical. Neither is selected or qualified. No extra cloud, custom FM, cross-cloud key chain or SKR is proposed solely for expiry. Funding, procurement, vendor engagement, resource reservation, installation, fault/destruction execution and final availability are distinct approval scopes.

The original ledger’s 661 decision null paths and 165 command-binding null paths remain unchanged. None of this table supplies a principal, credential, installation hash, executor, approval, resource reservation or receipt. Complete acceptance still depends on all required fields, not merely on the absence of questions in this table. C01–C16 remain: source; current/embedded snapshots; older versions; projections; caches; broker/outbox/DLQ/replay; spools/exports; telemetry; replicas; native backups; off-site/immutable copies; raw/wrapped/escrowed/rotated keys; key backups/seal dependencies; drill/quarantine copies; work/receipts; F1 continuations.
