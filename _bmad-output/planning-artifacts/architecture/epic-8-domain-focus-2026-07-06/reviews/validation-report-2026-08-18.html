ADV-1

Five deferral executors share one references/Hexalith.EventStore gitlink and one Builds catalog version, but I4/§4.1 record identity per story. Executor A (8.7) proves parity at EventStore 3.97.0 and deletes its rollback surface; executor B (8.8) bumps to 3.98.0 and refreshes only its own rows — both letter-compliant, and A's deletion now stands on evidence never run at the consumed identity. The 8.3 matrix already documents this failure shape twice.

Close with I16: parity evidence is valid only at the identity it was produced against; any retained-identity change re-opens every parity claim stamped at a different identity; no deletion merges into a tree whose identity differs from its evidence's stamp.
