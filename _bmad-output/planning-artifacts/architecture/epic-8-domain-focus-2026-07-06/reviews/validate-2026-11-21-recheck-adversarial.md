Following the spine can withdraw writable replicas during projection lag. Following the overview can admit a replica when its configured Tenants dependency is unavailable. I1a asks for topology parity but does not enumerate these cases. Correct both accounts and require G1/G2 cutover evidence for enabled and disabled Tenants, command dependencies and degradation of read dependencies. Keep catalog enrollment's separate startup requirement.
