## Selected solutions and limits

**Operational recovery:** prefer evidence of a legitimately current accepted profile if one exists. Otherwise prepare a bounded successor credential/profile and guarded consumer cutover after preservation reconciliation. Preserve old receipts and expired inputs. Do not renew expired credentials, replay installers or turn a new snapshot into historical proof. Keep both the Secret metadata blocker and the separately recorded historical Agents-register mismatch for their responsible owners; do not edit the register.

**Local cleanup:** select independent cleanup scheduling, explicit treatment of `openbao.snap` as well as dated files, recorded deletion outcomes and failure alerts as the candidate correction. Prepare it only after the actual inventory is available. The last usable backup must not be retained indefinitely by accident or removed without an accepted recovery tradeoff. File unlink alone is not all-copy physical erasure evidence.

**Scaleway disposal:** select shorter retention with margin and verified exact-version deletion as the first candidate to assess. Reject a bare 31-to-30-day lifecycle change as sufficient compliance. Keep the existing lock and lifecycle untouched until the owner can review concrete exact changes. Include current/noncurrent versions, legal holds, incomplete uploads, exceptional copies, local latest snapshots and every key-recovery route. Existing immutable versions require a transition plan; no assumption that changing a default shortens their lock.

For each key-bearing copy require `C + L + M <= E + 2,592,000 seconds`, where C is its latest possible capture time, L its immutable retention and M the disposal/verification margin. Post-expiry capture and retry/outage behavior must be included. If another workload requires 30-day immutable recovery of the same whole-store snapshot, preserve that requirement and record the conflict. A Transit mount is not a separate backup boundary. No shared seal/key destruction that harms unrelated live units is accepted. Unknown disposal stays Pending; missed bounds retain breach outcomes.

**Independent authority/time:** reuse an existing qualifying service only. Same-node HMAC, another local pod/PVC, an accurate clock alone and an immutable bucket alone do not establish current nonrollback authority. No custom consensus service, bucket-based authority, new cluster, hardware or extra cloud is commissioned. AWS remains excluded. Required time uncertainty remains at most 100 ms and observation age at most one second, with current fence/revocation checks and denial on uncertainty.

**Encryption:** retain direct Transit as the initial assessment mode for small history units, with independently erasable original-unit key families and all-generation coverage. Envelope encryption and an additional cryptographic layer around snapshots remain deferred unless measurements or an already qualified existing capability justify their added key holders. Direct Transit is not selected as a qualified production backend by this decision.

**Capacity and people:** use existing monitoring and a small workload sheet first: retained original units/generations, peak read/write rates, payload sizes, backup/copy growth, expiry bursts, measured latency and cleanup backlog/drain margin. Every number needs a source, observation time and owner; unknowns remain unknown. No benchmark framework or production load is commissioned. The implementer and operational recovery lead may be one appropriately appointed person, with a different Human deputy; independent authority administration remains separately evidenced. No person is appointed by inference from an Administrator or validator label.
